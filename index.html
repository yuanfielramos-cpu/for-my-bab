A letter for you

Hi my love!

Happy 730 days! Isn’t it crazy how time flew that fast, na 17 544 hours has passed since eomma and abeoji said we can finally be official?!?! Ever since our beginning, each day that passes reminds me of how lucky and blessed I am to have found my one.

Mahal, thank you for all the love you give me. Thank you mahal, na ang tanging rason lang kung bakit sumasakit ang puso ko ay dahil lang sa distansyang pumapagitan sa ating dalawa. Whether it’s a cloudy or sunny day to be together, I am so thankful that I get to share my life with you. Love, basta tayo, parang lahat napaka special- like our trip to Rusell! Even though it wasn’t even a big city, for me that trip reminds me of our love for each other. We don’t need big gestures to enjoy each other’s company or show our affection kase simpleng kape lang at kulitan and it’s a very special day.

Hanggang ngayon, napapaisip pa din ako, at tanging namamangha kung pano tayo naging. Dahil ikaw nuon, ikaw yung bituin na hinahanap ko to make a wish. Ngayon, ikaw na ang araw na nagbibigay ng saya, Liwanag, at ng kulay sa mundo ko. Love, you inspire me and make me feel a way that no else can. As we have grown together and I’ve been by your side, hindi ko mapigilan ang sarili ko na mas mahulog pa para sayo. And when I say that, it’s not just the kilig I’m talking about. When I look in your eyes, it’s like handang handa ako na ibigay ang lahat, na gawin ang lahat, so that I can show you how much I appreciate and love you.

I love you. And I wish that in our next year and forever, we would continue to grow in love together. Thank you mahal for being my greatest supporter, and bestfriend, and lover, and my everything. I promise to choose you and cherish us forever. Happy two years, my tinatangi.

your bab,
